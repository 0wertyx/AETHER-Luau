# AETHER peer visuals
Sync the Rojo project into an experience you own. The ServerScriptService/AetherPeerVisuals script creates AetherPeerVisualsV1 in ReplicatedStorage. In AETHER, open Interaction and enable Share and see AETHER cosmetics on both clients. Test with Studio's multi-client test. No outside backend, asset publishing, persistent account database or cross-server lookup is needed.
Only opted-in clients receive other opted-in players' cosmetic settings: halos, wings, neon outline, aura/trail, decorative diamonds and motion settings/props. The observing client reconstructs effects and estimates procedural locomotion from the replicated character movement, so animation phase may differ. Published Animator tracks are left alone. HUD, cursor, keybinds, world lighting, personal data and scripts remain local.
Quality limits: Low 4 nearby peers at 15Hz, Balanced 8 at 30Hz, High 12 at 60Hz; distant effects cap at 15Hz. A change-only snapshot plus an 8-second heartbeat is sent, with server rate limiting and expiry. Geometry is reused while its structural settings do not change; joint lookup scans the rig once. Actual FPS and bandwidth must be measured in Studio.
The standalone client does not create a server relay in third-party experiences. It reports unavailable there.
