# AETHER peer visuals
Sync the Rojo project into an experience you own. The ServerScriptService/AetherPeerVisuals script creates AetherPeerVisualsV1 in ReplicatedStorage. In AETHER, open Interaction and enable Share and see AETHER cosmetics on both clients. Test with Studio's multi-client test. No outside backend, asset publishing, persistent account database or cross-server lookup is needed.
Only opted-in clients receive other opted-in players' cosmetic settings: halos, wings, neon outline, aura/trail, decorative diamonds and motion settings/props. The observing client reconstructs effects and estimates procedural locomotion from the replicated character movement, so animation phase may differ. Published Animator tracks are left alone. HUD, cursor, keybinds, world lighting, personal data and scripts remain local.
Quality limits: Low 4 nearby peers at 15Hz, Balanced 8 at 30Hz, High 12 at 60Hz; distant effects cap at 15Hz. A change-only snapshot plus an 8-second heartbeat is sent, with server rate limiting and expiry. Geometry is reused while its structural settings do not change; joint lookup scans the rig once. Actual FPS and bandwidth must be measured in Studio.
The standalone client does not create a server relay in third-party experiences. It reports unavailable there.

## Connection recovery
The client distinguishes opt-in from an acknowledged connection. It retries absent relays every two seconds, retries unacknowledged initial shares, and reconnects if the remote disappears or a v2 acknowledgement is absent for 20 seconds. The updated server acknowledges every accepted share, including heartbeats. Older servers remain compatible with the initial ready message but do not support heartbeat acknowledgement monitoring. Desktop relay and in-place relay are separate; a missing server in an unrelated place is not fixed by installing this LocalScript alone.

## Air Tilt (protocol v3)
The sender shares only its quantized cosmetic root-joint pose, at up to 10 Hz while tilted. The server validates integer angles, limits update rate and forwards poses only between opted-in participants in the same authorized experience. Observers smooth and layer the pose locally on existing animation without changing physical movement; stale poses decay after 0.6 seconds and cannot carry across respawns. A v3 server and updated clients are required for this feature; v1/v2 servers retain their existing cosmetic exchange. Test with two Studio clients: opt both in, jump and tilt, verify observer pose, land, respawn, opt out and check pose cleanup. Not available in experiences without the authorized relay.
